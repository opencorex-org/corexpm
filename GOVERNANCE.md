# Governance

CorexPM begins with a lightweight maintainer model and will evolve as the
contributor community grows.

## Roles

- **Contributors** propose changes and participate in review.
- **Reviewers** are trusted contributors who can approve changes in documented
  areas.
- **Maintainers** merge releases, manage security reports, and steward project
  direction.

Roles are earned through sustained, constructive participation. Maintainers
document additions and removals publicly where privacy and safety allow.

## Decisions

Routine decisions happen in pull requests. User-visible semantics, persistent
formats, trust boundaries, and major architecture changes require an RFC.
Maintainers aim for consensus; when consensus is not possible, the responsible
maintainer records the decision and rationale.

Stable formats and APIs follow the compatibility policy. Security concerns can
override normal compatibility guarantees when documented with migration
guidance.

## Releases

At least one maintainer reviews a pre-1.0 release. Stable releases should
require two maintainers, green required checks, a changelog, signed artifacts,
published checksums, and a completed release checklist.
